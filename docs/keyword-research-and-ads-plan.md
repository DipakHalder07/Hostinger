## How this research was done

- **Google Autocomplete (India, English).** I pulled live suggestions for 16 seed queries. These are real searches people type, not guesses.
- **SERP and competitor check.** I looked at who ranks and advertises for "website design company in Siliguri", and what prices they publish.
- **Your own site (digitalgrove.in).** I used your services, portfolio, client reviews and contact details. The landing page uses only facts that are already on your site.

> **Volumes:** Siliguri is a small market. Most of these keywords probably get **10–200 searches a month each** in Google Keyword Planner. Before you set budgets, check the numbers in Google Ads → Tools → Keyword Planner (location: Siliguri + 25 km). Because volume is low, use **phrase match and exact match only**, plus a tight negative keyword list. Broad match will spend your money on job seekers and course searches.

## 3. Google Ads campaign structure

**Campaign:** `Search | Website Design | Siliguri`
- **Location:** Siliguri + 25 km radius (covers Bagdogra, Matigara, Jalpaiguri edge). Set Location options to **"Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations"**, not "interest".
- **Languages:** English, Hindi, Bengali
- **Bidding:** Start with **Maximize Clicks with a CPC cap of ₹25–40** for 2–3 weeks, or until you have about 15 conversions. Then switch to **Maximize Conversions** and later **Target CPA**.
- **Budget:** start at ₹500–800/day, then reallocate to whichever ad groups convert.
- **Schedule:** Mon–Sat 9am–9pm to start (calls are answered). Add Sunday once you can handle WhatsApp replies on Sundays.
- **Networks:** turn **off** the Display Network and Search Partners.

## 5. Conversion tracking (set this up before you spend)

The page already sends these events to `window.dataLayer`. Use them in Google Tag Manager:

| Event | Fires when | Use as |
|---|---|---|
| `generate_lead` | Form submitted successfully | **Primary** Google Ads conversion |
| `click_whatsapp` | Any WhatsApp button tapped | Secondary conversion |
| `click_call` | Any tel: link tapped | Secondary conversion (or use Ads call tracking) |
| `cta_click` | Any "Get quote" button | Micro-conversion (observation only) |

The thank-you page (`thank-you.html`) also has a commented-out `gtag` conversion snippet. Paste your Conversion ID and label there if you don't use GTM.

**UTM parameters and `gclid`** are captured from the URL and sent to HubSpot with each lead, so you can see which keyword each lead came from.

Use this Google Ads tracking template so HubSpot shows keyword-level source data:
```
{lpurl}?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={campaignid}&utm_term={keyword}&utm_content={creative}
```

## 6. Ongoing CRO checklist

- [ ] Reply to every form lead or WhatsApp message within 15 minutes during ad hours. Speed matters more than anything else on this list.
- [ ] Check the Search Terms report weekly and add new negatives.
- [ ] A/B test the hero headline after you have about 100 clicks. Example variant: "Get More Enquiries With a Website Built in Siliguri"
- [ ] Add real Google review count/stars to the hero once your Google Business Profile has 10+ reviews.
- [ ] Add 1–2 more Siliguri case studies with a measurable result (e.g. "+40% direct bookings").
- [ ] Keep prices on the page current. Wrong prices hurt trust and Ads policy compliance.
