Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how Digital Grove ("we", "us"), a website design and digital marketing business based in Siliguri, West Bengal, India, collects and uses personal information when you visit our website or contact us. By using our website or submitting an enquiry, you agree to this policy.

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies from services such as HubSpot, Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) to measure visits and enquiries and to show relevant ads. We share information only with service providers who help us run our business, for example our CRM and form provider (HubSpot), analytics and advertising platforms (Google, Meta), and messaging and email services (WhatsApp, Gmail). They may store data on servers outside India. We may also share information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and provide our services, and for any period required by law. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask us to access, correct, update or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent to be contacted. To do this, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.
