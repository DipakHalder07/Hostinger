Website design in Siliguri: FAQs

Answers to the questions Siliguri business owners ask us most.

How much does website design cost in Siliguri?

A simple business website with Digital Grove starts at ₹9,999. A full business website with SEO setup starts at ₹19,999, and an ecommerce or Shopify store starts at ₹34,999. You get a fixed written quote before any work starts, with no hidden charges.

How long does it take to build a website?

A starter website is usually ready in 7–10 working days once we have your content. Business websites take about 2–3 weeks and ecommerce stores about 3–5 weeks, depending on the number of products and pages.

Will my website show up on Google?

Yes. Every website is built SEO-ready: fast loading, mobile-friendly, with proper titles, meta descriptions, headings, schema markup and a sitemap. We also submit it to Google Search Console and help set up or optimise your Google Business Profile so you appear in local Siliguri searches.

Do I need to buy a domain and hosting separately?

No. We can register the domain and set up secure hosting with SSL for you, in your name, so you fully own them. If you already have a domain or hosting, we will use it.

Can I update the website myself later?

Yes. We build on easy-to-use platforms such as HubSpot CMS, WordPress or Shopify and give you a short training session so you can change text, photos, prices and products yourself. We also offer maintenance plans if you prefer us to handle it.

Do you build ecommerce websites and online stores?

Yes. We have built online stores for tea brands such as TeaAru, The Tea Cartel and Nidhi Tea, with product catalogues, UPI and card payments, shipping and order management.

Do you only work with businesses in Siliguri?

We are a Siliguri-based team and most of our clients are in Siliguri, Darjeeling, Jalpaiguri and Sikkim, but we work with businesses anywhere in India over phone, WhatsApp and video calls.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We review your current site, find what is slowing it down or losing enquiries, and rebuild it with a modern design, faster loading and better calls-to-action while keeping your existing Google rankings safe with proper redirects.
